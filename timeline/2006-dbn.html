2006

Deep belief nets and greedy layer-wise pretraining

Key people: Hinton, Osindero, Teh; Bengio, Lamblin, Popovici, Larochelle

Why it matters

This is the “deep learning revival.” The key idea: train deep models layer by layer, often unsupervised, then fine-tune. It made depth feel trainable again before the GPU/ImageNet era.
