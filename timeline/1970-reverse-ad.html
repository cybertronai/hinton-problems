1970

Reverse-mode automatic differentiation

Key people: Seppo Linnainmaa

Why it matters

Modern backprop is essentially reverse-mode differentiation applied to layered computations. Schmidhuber’s historical account credits Linnainmaa’s 1970 work as the first publication of modern reverse-mode AD.
