Late 1980s–1990s

Convolutional neural networks for vision

Key people: Yann LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, Haffner, others

Why it matters

Backprop meets weight sharing and locality. CNNs become the architecture for images, handwriting, and eventually computer vision. LeCun’s 1989 work on backprop for handwritten zip-code recognition is an early practical anchor.
