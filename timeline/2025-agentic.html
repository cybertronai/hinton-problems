Unified multimodal/agentic systems

Key people: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, xAI, DeepSeek, open-source ecosystem

Why it matters

The frontier shifts from “a model” to “a system”: multimodal input/output, tool use, code execution, retrieval, memory, agents, and workflow automation. OpenAI’s GPT-5 and GPT-5.5 releases frame this as a move toward unified systems that can do useful work across modalities and tasks.
