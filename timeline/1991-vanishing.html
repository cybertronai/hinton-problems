1991

Vanishing-gradient problem identified

Key people: Sepp Hochreiter, later Bengio et al.

Why it matters

Deep and recurrent nets are theoretically trainable, but gradients vanish or explode over long chains. This explains why “depth” and “memory” remained hard. Bengio’s 1994 work is a classic treatment of why learning long-term dependencies with gradient descent is difficult.
